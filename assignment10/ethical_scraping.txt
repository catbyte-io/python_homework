The purpose of robots.txt is to specify which parts of a website are allowed for accessing and scraping and the user-agents that can be used.
Rate limits may also be specified in robots.txt.
This file promotes ethical scraping by deterring scrapers from paths containing sensitive or protected information and content.
It also specifies how web scrapers can get content without making that content unavailable to other users.
When the guidelines in robots.txt are followed, it helps keep scraping practices within regulations and policies.
This also maintains responsible data collection practices and promotes data availability.
